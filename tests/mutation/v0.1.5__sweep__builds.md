## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. **A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** No test runs the real Windows "exe in use" refusal: it is simulated by a stubbed `os.replace`, and the live check is djdarcy's.

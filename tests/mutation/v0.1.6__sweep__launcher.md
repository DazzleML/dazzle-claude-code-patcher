## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. **A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** No test runs a real exe or a real shim. Real shell resolution of `claude`, Ctrl+C through `claude.cmd`, and exit codes through each shim are djdarcy's checklist.
